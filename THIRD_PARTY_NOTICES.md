# Asset and dependency boundaries

- Tabler icons under `student/frontend/public/visual/icons`: Paweł Kuna and Tabler Icons contributors, MIT. The license notice is included in `visual/licenses/MIT.txt`.
- Decorative backgrounds and badge: supplied in the project's visual-assets package, with source-supplied CC0 provenance preserved in `visual/manifest.json`. This is provenance documentation, not an independent legal certification.
- Car preview: source attribution is retained in `visual/ATTRIBUTION.md` and `visual/credits.txt`.
- PerminIA logo and brand files were provided for this project; no permission to reuse the brand as another product is granted here.
- Synthetic shapes and walkthrough text are authored in `student/backend/seed_showcase.py`; they contain no scraped driving-law questions, media or official exam claims.
- Imported lesson/question banks, associated audio/video, private reports and database exports are deliberately excluded from the public handoff.
- React, Vite, FastAPI, PostgreSQL and other dependencies retain their own licenses. JavaScript and Python version/lock files identify the dependencies; bundled asset notices do not license the original application as a whole.

Before widening redistribution or choosing a project-wide license, the project owner should confirm the rights to every included brand/decorative asset. The public release tool excludes the private dataset; it is not a copyright audit.
